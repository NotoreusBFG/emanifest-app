# Quiz reference (DRAFT — 10 questions, suggested pass threshold 80%)

Every question below is answered directly by one of the three module
scripts in this folder — see the "Quiz tie-in" notes inline in each script
for exactly where. Keep this file in sync if a script's examples change.

1. **What EPA form number is the Uniform Hazardous Waste Manifest?**
   Answer: 8700-22 — *Module 1, Scene 2*

2. **Signing Item 15 certifies the shipment is properly classed, packed, marked, and labeled per which DOT sections?**
   Answer: 172.204/172.205 — *Module 2, Scene 2*

3. **True/False: A Large Quantity Generator's Item 15 signature also certifies a waste minimization program under 40 CFR 262.27(a).**
   Answer: True — *Module 2, Scenes 2-3*

4. **If you sign as a delegate under a Quick-Sign delegation, whose certification are you legally making?**
   Answer: The owner's/account holder's — not your own independent certification — *Module 2, Scene 4*

5. **What DOT regulation triggers hazmat employee training for anyone who signs the manifest?**
   Answer: 49 CFR 172.704 — *Module 1, Scene 1*

6. **How often must recurrent DOT hazmat employee training be completed?**
   Answer: Every 3 years — *Module 1, Scene 4*

7. **True/False: This course satisfies DOT's safety-training component of 172.704.**
   Answer: False — safety training must be facility/material-specific — *Module 3, Scene 7*

8. **Before releasing a hazmat shipment to a transporter, what should you verify?**
   Answer: That the driver/vehicle matches who was actually expected — *Module 3, Scene 4*

9. **What federal statute carries potential criminal liability for a knowing false statement on a RCRA-required document like the manifest?**
   Answer: 42 U.S.C. §6928(d) — *Module 2, Scene 5*

10. **True/False: In-depth security training under 172.802 applies to every generator regardless of size.**
    Answer: False — only generators required to have a DOT security plan — *Module 3, Scene 6*
